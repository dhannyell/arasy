The fire, smoke, pollen, and rain particles used by the example come from Kenney's [Particle
Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack). The pack is released under
[Creative Commons CC0](https://creativecommons.org/public-domain/cc0/).

Files used by this demo:
